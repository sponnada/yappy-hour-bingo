Privacy

Yappy Hour Bingo is a static web app. It does not require an account, sign-in, or authentication.

What is stored

The randomized card, first names entered on squares, and completed-line state are saved only in this browser’s local storage so progress can survive a refresh.

What is transmitted

The app has no backend, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party API calls. Game entries are not transmitted by the app.

How to remove data

Use Clear names in the game or clear site data in the browser. Because storage is device- and browser-specific, entries do not follow a participant to another device.

Hosting logs

A public hosting provider may keep standard request logs under its own policies. Those logs are outside the app and do not include bingo entries stored in local storage.
